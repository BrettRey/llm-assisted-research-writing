# LLM-assisted research writing

A research project by Brett Reynolds examining the development of research claims during sustained writing with multiple large language models.

The proposed study follows a linguistics project through changes in its questions, arguments, evidence and drafts. Its provisional unit of analysis is the history of a claim: what was proposed, how it was challenged, what evidence was checked, what changed, and whether that change persisted.

**Status:** Project established; study design pending and no manuscript yet.
